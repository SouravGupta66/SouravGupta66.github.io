About

Research-driven engineer in computer vision

I'm a Trainee AI Engineer (Computer Vision & Edge AI) at SmartData Technologies and Information & Communication Engineering student at Bangladesh University of Professionals.

At work I build real-time vision pipelines that run directly on low-power edge NPUs like the DEEPX DX-M1, so video analytics stays on-premise with no cloud dependency. That includes Smart Office, a multi-camera workplace analytics platform in C++.

My research centres on trustworthy medical image analysis: data leakage and domain shift in CT classification, parameter-efficient adaptation of vision-language models for chest X-rays, and visual grounding in 3D clinical VLMs. My thesis on CT lung cancer classification is published at ACM ICCA 2026.

Competitive programming (Codeforces rating 1033) taught me to break problems down. I enjoy researching real-world applications of AI and collaborating on scalable technology.

Research

Research & publications

Trustworthy medical image analysis: leakage, domain shift and what models actually look at.

01

Published

Classical Preprocessing Does Not Close the Domain Gap: A Multi-Architecture, Leakage-Controlled Study on CT Lung Cancer Classification

ACM ICCA 2026 — 4th International Conference on Computing Advancements, ACM Digital Library · Author & B.Sc. thesis

Audited two popular CT datasets (IQ-OTH and Mendeley) and found 416 pixel-verified duplicate images that inflated evaluation metrics by +0.2163 AUC. Statistical (Wilcoxon) and feature-space (MMD) analysis across 3 architectures shows classical preprocessing fails to bridge the domain divergence.

Data leakageDomain shift (MMD)CT imaging

02

Under review

Efficient Adaptation of MedCLIP for Multi-Label Chest X-ray Classification via Low-Rank Adaptation on MIMIC-CXR

ICCIT 2026 — International Conference on Computer and Information Technology · Author

LoRA adaptation of the MedCLIP vision-language model under a strict patient-disjoint split matches deep-layer fine-tuning (0.7980 test Macro-AUC) while updating 95.3% fewer parameters, cutting optimizer memory 21× and narrowing the train–test gap 2.5×.

Vision-language modelsLoRAChest X-ray

Visual Grounding Evaluation in 3D CT Vision-Language Models

Supervisor: Atika Akter, PhD Candidate, IBMI, Helmholtz Munich

Testing whether MedGemma 1.5, a clinical VLM, grounds its predictions in 3D CT evidence or relies on text priors, via controlled ablations (image removal, spatial scrambling, patient-swap) on the public CT-RATE dataset. Built a memory-efficient pipeline to run 4B-parameter multimodal models on a single GPU.
